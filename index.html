How much do the rentals cost?

It depends on the items, quantity, date and venue. Send your list and we'll give you a full quote.

Can I see the items before I book?

Yes. Visit our showroom in East Legon. Call ahead so someone can walk you through.

Do you deliver and set up?

Tell us your venue when you send your list, and delivery and setup will be included in your quote.

How early should I book?

As early as you can, especially for weekend weddings. Send your date and we'll check availability.
